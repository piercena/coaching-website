The Core Method

Break the Loop of Over-Analysis. Choose Forward.

Being stuck rarely looks like standing still. More often, it looks like chaotic motion in a circle. Endless planning, endless refining, and analyzing past decisions to figure out where momentum was lost.

Reflection has value, but only when it informs your next choice. Progress doesn't require complete certainty; it requires a decision made from what you know right now.
